# Naming

- Skill directories are bare kebab-case slugs. The `cortex:` namespace comes from `plugin.json`.
- Scripts are `scripts/cortex_<name>.py`. Receipts are `.cortex-<name>-receipt.json`. Settings stamps are `cortex<Name>`. Commit trailers are `Cortex-<Name>`.
- The `ai-brain vault for this project:` line in a cycle's `CLAUDE.md` is parsed by scripts. Do not reword it.
- Branch prefixes are `feat`, `fix` and `chore`.
